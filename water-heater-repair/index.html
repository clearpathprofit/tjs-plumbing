Signs your water heater needs attention

  • The hot water runs out faster than it used to
  • Rumbling or popping sounds from the tank
  • Rusty or discolored hot water
  • Water pooling around the base of the tank
  • No hot water at all

A leak from the bottom of the tank is the one that can't wait. Shut off the water supply to the heater and call.

Repair or replace?

Sometimes a part fixes it. Sometimes the tank is done. TJ will tell you which, and you'll get a free written quote before any work starts.

Traditional or tankless

TJ works on both. A tank heater keeps a supply of hot water ready. A tankless unit heats water as you use it and mounts on the wall. Ask which fits your house and how you use hot water.
