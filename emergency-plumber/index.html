What counts as a plumbing emergency

  • A burst or leaking pipe, or water coming through a ceiling
  • A flooding basement or a sump pump that has stopped running
  • A sewer or drain backup into the house
  • No water, or a water heater leaking from the bottom
  • Frozen pipes in a Rochester cold snap

While you wait

  • Shut off the main water valve. It is usually in the basement where the water line comes through the wall, near the meter.
  • Open a faucet on the lowest floor to drain water still in the pipes.
  • Keep people and electronics away from standing water. If water is near outlets or the panel, stay out and say so when you call.
  • Move what you can off the floor and take photos for your insurance.

Frozen pipes

Keep the heat on, open cabinet doors under sinks on outside walls, and let a faucet drip on the coldest nights. If a pipe is already frozen, never use an open flame to thaw it. Call and TJ will tell you what to do next.
