Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2026. Last updated: November 4, 2026.

Information we collect

When you submit the contact form on tjsplumbingny.com, we collect your name, phone number, email address and any details you give us about your plumbing needs.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your service inquiry
  • To schedule and confirm appointments
  • To send service-related text messages and follow-ups, if you opted in
  • To request a Google review after a completed job

Text messages (SMS)

If you check the optional text-message box on our form, you agree to receive texts from TJ's Plumbing about your inquiry, appointments and follow-ups. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out at any time. Consent is not a condition of service.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-message opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

Data sharing

We do not sell, trade or share your personal information with third parties except as required to provide our services or comply with the law.

Data retention

We keep your information only as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal obligations.
