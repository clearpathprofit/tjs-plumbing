When to call

  • A sink, tub or shower that drains slower every week
  • Gurgling from drains or the toilet
  • Water backing up in more than one drain at once
  • Sewage smell or water coming up through a floor drain

More than one drain backing up at the same time usually points to the main line, not a single clog. That's worth a call right away.

Main line and sewer problems

For problems deeper in the line, TJ does camera inspections, hydro-jetting and sewer line repair, and finds the problem without digging up your yard.

Questions people ask

Do chemical drain cleaners work?

For a stubborn or repeat clog, they often don't reach the cause. If a drain keeps coming back slow, have it cleared properly.

Can you inspect the sewer line?

Yes. TJ does camera inspections to find what's causing a backup.
