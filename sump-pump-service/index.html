Test your sump pump before spring

Snowmelt and spring rain are when a pump earns its keep. A quick check before then:

  • Slowly pour a bucket of water into the pit. The pump should start, empty the pit and shut off.
  • Make sure it's plugged in and the cord isn't damaged.
  • Check that the discharge line outside isn't blocked or frozen.

Call if you notice

  • The pump runs constantly, or never turns on
  • Loud grinding or rattling
  • Water in the basement after rain
